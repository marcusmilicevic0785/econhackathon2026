# Data rights and attribution

This document describes source terms; it does not grant rights beyond those of each provider. No software license has been selected for the original project code.

| Material | Attribution / terms | Distribution decision |
|---|---|---|
| BLS ATUS public-use data | U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, American Time Use Survey; [BLS copyright](https://www.bls.gov/bls/copyright.htm) | Share aggregate results; official ZIP download script and checksum provided |
| Census ACS, Gazetteer, CBP, BFS | U.S. Census Bureau; federal government data in public domain; [reuse](https://www.census.gov/about/policies/quality/guidelines.html) | Include relevant extracts; preserve official source URLs and definitions |
| CDC PLACES | Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, PLACES release/reference year recorded; [CDC data policy](https://www.cdc.gov/other/agencymaterials.html) | Include requested public aggregate records and provenance; modeled-estimate limitations apply |
| NYC Facilities/libraries/parks | NYC DCP, NYC Parks, and library agencies; [NYC Open Data terms](https://opendata.cityofnewyork.us/overview/#termsofuse) | Public source snapshots with attribution and as-is caveats; no agency endorsement |
| Third Place Index | Evan O’Neil, National Third Place Index; [methodology/terms](https://thirdplaceindex.org/methodology/); OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) | Local download only while compiled-index permissions remain unclear; do not bundle row-level TPI data or claim public-domain status for the compiled index |
| Source notes and past event pages | User-supplied notes; organizer pages cited in guides | Research summarized in original wording; private organizer logistics and full third-party text not redistributed |

Keep this file, the manifests, and model source notes with redistributed data/results. No government seal, endorsement, or certification is implied. Individual privacy-sensitive records beyond already public survey microdata are not collected.
